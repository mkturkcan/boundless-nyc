> Ground-floor retail with 1–6 floors above, 1890–1940 masonry + purpose-built "taxpayers": 125th St, Lenox/Malcolm X Blvd, 116th St, Bedford Ave, Grand St, Broadway (Bushwick/Williamsburg under the J/M/Z), Fulton St, 3rd Ave.

### 1.2 Frontage subdivision rule (retail bay assignment)
Assign retail frontage by sampling a **store module count** across a 7.62 m lot:

| Case | Storefront clear opening each | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| A — 1 store per lot | 6.10–7.01 m (20–23 ft) | 0.44 |
| B — 2 stores per lot | 3.35–3.81 m (11–12.5 ft) each | 0.20 |
| C — 1 store spans 2 lots (15.24 m) | 13.41–14.63 m (44–48 ft), with 1–2 intermediate piers | 0.20 |
| D — 3 stores per lot (narrow: nail salon / phone repair / barber) | 2.29–2.59 m (7.5–8.5 ft) each | 0.06 |
| E — 1 store spans 3+ lots (22.86 m+, supermarket/pharmacy) | 20.4–22.3 m | 0.06 |
| F — retail vacant / gated / papered over | any of above | 0.04 |

**Hard rule:** every mixed-use building with residential above must ALSO surrender **1.07–1.68 m (42–66 in)** of frontage to a separate residential entry (§3.9). Subtract this from available retail frontage BEFORE subdividing. Corner buildings put the residential door on the side street 60% of the time.

Party-wall pier / pilaster between adjacent storefronts: **0.20–0.61 m (8–24 in)** wide, typ **0.30 m (12 in)**. Intermediate structural pier inside a wide store: **0.20–0.30 m (8–12 in)** cast-iron column (pre-1900) or **0.30–0.41 m (12–16 in)** brick pier (taxpayer).

### 1.5 Setbacks & upper massing
- **No setback** on 3–6 story buildings in C1/C2 overlays — straight wall from sidewalk to parapet.
- Taxpayer 1–2 story: no setback; the parapet is the whole "cap."
- Post-1961 infill (rare here, weight 0.05): sky-exposure-plane setback at 18.29 m (60 ft) on a 30.48 m (100 ft) wide street, depth 3.05–4.57 m.
- Rear: retail one-story extension covers the rear yard to lot line — visible as a **flat roof at 4.11–4.57 m** behind the main block, often with condensers and a skylight (1.22 x 1.83 m).

### 3.8 Corner conditions
- Chamfered corner entry: chamfer face **1.52–2.44 m** wide at 45°, door centered, on 55% of corner buildings.

- Corner store gets **2 sign bands** (one per elevation) that meet at the corner and usually mis-align vertically by **0.03–0.10 m**.
